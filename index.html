How scoring works

Each axis has six statements. Strong agreement and disagreement count for two points. Agreement and disagreement count for one. Neutral counts as zero. Some statements are scored in reverse so agreement can move the score toward either end of an axis.

Each total is divided by the maximum possible for the statements you scored, then scaled from −100 to +100. The left and lower ends are negative. Skipped answers are excluded. An axis appears once you score at least three statements on it.

A score near the middle can reflect consistently moderate answers or strong views that pull in different directions. Use your answer review to see which statements mattered to you.
